Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about editing PDFs at pdf-editor.oriohub.com.

Is OrioHub PDF Editor completely free and private?

Yes. OrioHub PDF Editor (pdf-editor.oriohub.com) runs 100% locally inside your web browser using PDF.js and pdf-lib. Your PDF files and images are never uploaded to any remote server.

How do I edit existing text inside a PDF file?

Select the Whiteout tool in the top toolbar and tap/click over the existing text to place a white mask. Drag its handles to cover the old text, then switch to Add Text to type your replacement text right on top.

Can I add images, position text accurately, and reorder pages on mobile phones?

Yes! OrioHub PDF Editor automatically fits PDFs to your smartphone screen, supports smooth touch dragging without scrolling the page, includes a 4-way Nudge D-Pad for pixel-accurate positioning, and provides Undo/Redo buttons.
